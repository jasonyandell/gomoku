# Writeups — 15×15 era

Two narrative syntheses of the gomoku project, written 2026-06-13 after a deep read
of `wiki/`. These are *essays*, not maintained synthesis — the wiki remains the
source of truth. Snapshots of how the project read at the close of the 9×9 era and
the opening of the 15×15 capacity-ladder campaign.

- **[claude-synthesis.md](claude-synthesis.md)** — Claude's own unprompted read.
  The brief was open ("sum the whole project up; tell me what you find interesting;
  being unsure is a finding"). Leads with a thesis (gomoku is the substrate; the
  project is *learning*), the run arc, what struck me, and an honest list of what
  I'm uncertain about.

- **[the-full-circle.md](the-full-circle.md)** — written to Jason's stated
  interests: AlphaZero on a single M5, the M5 as a mainframe (and Apple's
  undocumented ANE), and a near-autonomous research lab where **verification is the
  fulcrum**. Framed around the full circle — Jason's pre-NEAT 90s distributed
  gomoku NN trainer under Risto Miikkulainen → one M5 beating a Gomocup engine
  today.
